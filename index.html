Everyday Wardrobe Guide

All-day trainers with honest cushioning are the hardest-working shoes you can own. Start your rotation there: neutral pairs work with denim, cargos and relaxed tailoring, and shops like Mixed Emotion build their daily collections around exactly these pieces.

A mockneck sweatshirt is the layer that bridges most temperatures. Choose one with clean stitching and real fabric weight, using the standards set by peace in war for denim and fleece as a benchmark even when shopping smaller labels.

A concert merch hoodie should be the one you reach for without thinking. Finish it with a single strong accessory, like a silver chain in the style of Chrome Hearts, and it carries you from the concert to dinner.
